## Lead (5 bullets)

1. **Cour cannot certify** CNAF / branche famille FY2024: residual errors after internal control at **24 months = €6.3bn (8% of benefits)** — *indus and underpayments that will never be regularised*; hot on RSA, *prime d’activité*, housing aids. [Cour publication](https://www.ccomptes.fr/fr/publications/certification-des-comptes-du-regime-general-de-securite-sociale-et-du-cpsti-exercice) · [full PDF p.63](https://www.ccomptes.fr/sites/default/files/2025-05/20250516-certification-comptes-securite-sociale-2024.pdf) · [press doc 75241](https://www.ccomptes.fr/fr/documents/75241)
2. **9-month residual risk:** Cour *summary* cites **€9.4bn**; full report gives **€8.4–10.3bn (10.5–12.9%)**, central **11.7%**. [Publication](https://www.ccomptes.fr/fr/publications/certification-des-comptes-du-regime-general-de-securite-sociale-et-du-cpsti-exercice) · [PDF pp.63, 70](https://www.ccomptes.fr/sites/default/files/2025-05/20250516-certification-comptes-securite-sociale-2024.pdf)
3. **Fraud ≠ error:** CNAF statistical fraud estimate for 2023 benefits **€3.8–4.7bn** (4.6–5.7%); detected intentional fraud **€449m (2024)** / **€508.8m (2025)** — ~9–12% of the estimate. [Cour PDF p.66](https://www.ccomptes.fr/sites/default/files/2025-05/20250516-certification-comptes-securite-sociale-2024.pdf) · [CNAF DP 2024](https://www.caf.fr/sites/default/files/medias/cnaf/250505%20DPLa%20lutte%20contre%20la%20fraude%20%C3%A0%20la%20Caf%20%C3%A9volue%20avec%20la%20solidarit%C3%A9%20%C3%A0%20la%20source%20et%20le%20SNLFE.pdf) · [CNAF DP 2025](https://www.caf.fr/sites/default/files/medias/cnaf/Nous_connaitre/Presse/2026/260415%20DP%20Lutte%20contre%20la%20fraude.pdf)
4. **HCFiPS (all social fraud):** evaluated **~€13bn**/yr; detected **€2.1bn** (incl. €0.5bn stopped); recovered **€0.6bn**; **CNAF = 30%** of evaluated total. [HCFiPS page](https://www.strategie-plan.gouv.fr/publications/hcfips-lutte-contre-fraude-sociale-etat-lieux-enjeux) (25 Sep 2024)
5. **Organised schemes exist in official record:** CNAF cites TJ Nice 2025 identity-usurpation ring — **€210k**, **29 CAF**, **4 years firm**; SNLFE flags *fraude à la résidence*, document fraud, false activity, platform income. [CNAF DP 2025 pp.6–8](https://www.caf.fr/sites/default/files/medias/cnaf/Nous_connaitre/Presse/2026/260415%20DP%20Lutte%20contre%20la%20fraude.pdf)

### B2. Cour — indus management (régime général, May 2025)

- Indus detected 2023 (4 branches): **€10.3bn**; undetected estimated **€8.6bn**; total ~**€18.9bn** (~5% of benefits). [RALFSS indus PDF](https://www.ccomptes.fr/sites/default/files/2025-05/20250526-RALFSS-2025-Gestion-indus-par-caisses-SS-du-regime-general.pdf)

- Fraudulent share of detected indus: **€0.7bn** (7%); higher among unpaid stock (**54%** for famille in Cour text). Same PDF.

### B3. Assemblée — government reply on 10% majoration

AN QE [17-4323](https://questions.assemblee-nationale.fr/q17/17-4323QE.htm) (reply 3 Jun 2025): LFSS 2023 art. 100 — 10% indemnity on fraudulent indus; applied by CAF from **1 Jan 2024**; CNAF 2024: **€28m** of the €449.2m prejudice figure is that 10% majoration.

### D4. CADA / CRPA (FOIA-equivalent)

- Law: *Code des relations entre le public et l’administration* **L311-1** et seq.; refusal → **CADA** opinion → admin court.
- Precedent: CADA [202205787](https://www.cada.fr/202205787) / related **20226179** — CNAF **risk-score algorithm**: **current** variables/coefficients **non-communicable** (fraud-prevention reserve, L311-5 2° g); **former** model versions communicable; RGPD register / DPIA generally communicable with occultations.
- Practical path for journalists: (1) written request to CNAF *personne responsable de l’accès aux documents*; (2) if silence/refusal → CADA within 2 months; (3) Ma Dada body page for CNAF requests: https://madada.fr/body/cnaf
- **Expect refusal** for: live datamining weights, open investigations, personal data of allocataires.
