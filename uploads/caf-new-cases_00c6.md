## Lead — 7 new cited cases / official findings

### 1. TJ Caen (correctionnel) — 6 May 2024 — identity usurpation
- **Scheme:** Chad national (44) stole Sudanese refugee’s identity; CAF benefits under false ID from **Mar 2023**; also Vitale card; caught when applying for stay title under real name in Caen (Jul 2023).
- **€:** Not stated in report.
- **Sentence:** **6 months prison suspended**; copy of judgment to be sent to CAF.
- **Source:** [Ouest-France](https://www.ouest-france.fr/normandie/caen-14000/juge-a-caen-pour-avoir-vole-lidentite-dun-refugie-soudanais-da0ab9ec-0e02-11ef-a10a-8d6450bd9eac) (court reporting). **Gap:** no judgment PDF / € amount.

### 2. TJ Meaux — 2 Dec 2025 — identity docs + false rent ads → CAF payments
- **Scheme:** Two brothers (~20s); fake Leboncoin rental ads; took victims’ ID; opened accounts; contacted CAF to collect benefits in victims’ names.
- **€:** ~**€5,000** paid by CAF Seine-et-Marne + CAF Seine-Saint-Denis.
- **Sentence:** each **15 months probationary suspended** + work obligation + repay Treasury; **€3,000** fine each.
- **Source:** [Ouest-France](https://www.ouest-france.fr/societe/faits-divers/seine-et-marne-deux-jeunes-freres-condamnes-pour-avoir-detourne-des-aides-sociales-5904289e-d122-11f0-9d24-18896e33f506) (citing Le Parisien / hearing). **Gap:** judgment PDF not retrieved.

### 3. TJ (Oise) — hearing 7 Sep 2026 — multi-CAF stolen identities
- **Scheme:** Man (27); Oct 2022–Jul 2025; harvested IDs online; claimed benefits + *attestations sur l’honneur*; **16** allocataire victims; hit CAF Var, Nord, Seine-et-Marne, Eure, Yvelines, Oise (Oise hardest hit).
- **€:** **>€21,000** diverted; ordered to repay **>€15,000** to CAF + **€145** to one allocataire.
- **Sentence:** **8 months firm** (electronic bracelet per réquisitions).
- **Source:** [Courrier picard](https://www.courrier-picard.fr/id743617/article/2026-09-08/un-homme-condamne-dans-loise-pour-avoir-detourne-21-000-euros-la-caf-grace-des). **Gap:** exact TJ seat / judgment PDF not in article.

### 4. TJ Draguignan — ~6–11 Oct 2025 — false leases + children abroad (résidence / docs)
- **Scheme:** Three brothers vs CAF Var (2014–2020). Hatem/Mohamed: family/schooling benefits while **10 children lived in Tunisia** (~€67k + ~€46k). Nouri: owner of building in Le Muy; **false leases/quittances** using names of people abroad (some unaware); tenant on undeveloped plot.
- **€:** Press totals ~**€150k** / “>€100k”; Nouri still owes CAF **€37,000**.
- **Sentence:** Hatem & Mohamed **6 months simple suspended** each; Nouri **18 months suspended** + **€10,000** fine.
- **Sources:** [Nice-Matin](https://www.nicematin.com/societe/justice/trois-freres-detournent-150-000-euros-de-prestations-sociales-aupres-de-la-caf-du-var-10651203); [Midi Libre](https://www.midilibre.fr/2025/10/11/faux-baux-enfants-vivant-a-letranger-trois-freres-escroquent-la-caf-pour-plus-de-100-000-euros-12986553.php). **Gap:** no CAF primary post / judgment PDF (was only a “secondary” footnote in prior memo — now full case entry).

### 5. TJ Châteauroux (pôle social) — 4 Sep 2025, RG 24/00118 — résidence abroad / fictitious FR address
- **Scheme:** CAF Indre vs allocataire (address Dakar). Undeclared stays **>3 months** in Senegal; airline + bank evidence; CAF: **fictitious address** used to claim FR residence for **AAH + majoration vie autonome**.
- **€:** Indu notified **€9,318.51** then **€12,343.62** (1 Sep 2021–30 Apr 2023); penalty **€130** + **10% majoration €1,234.36**.
- **Outcome:** Fraud upheld; pénalité + 10% confirmed; periods of fraudulent AAH/MVA fixed by court.
- **Source (primary judgment text):** [LibreJustice](https://librejustice.fr/decision/fr/NmRFqQO4jVNe) · also [courdecassation.fr decision id in page](https://www.courdecassation.fr/decision/69862042cdc6046d4738e483).

### 6. TJ Lyon (pôle social) — 20 Dec 2024, RG 23/02150 — false FR residence while abroad
- **Scheme:** Consular alert (Dakar): allocataire on French abroad register (previously Côte d’Ivoire) while declaring FR address to CAF; **AAH** (and contested RSA) while abroad.
- **€:** AAH indu notified **€13,585.62** (Mar 2021–May 2022); court orders balance **€4,710.70** + fraud penalty **€710**. (RSA track sent to admin court.)
- **Outcome:** Bad faith / fraud characterised (false residence declarations); pénalité upheld.
- **Source (primary):** [LibreJustice](https://librejustice.fr/decision/fr/tPOCvqblV-d0).

### 7. CAF Haute-Marne — 2023 controls published Jun 2024 — résidence IP + identity/RIB (local quantified pattern)
- **Résidence:** DTR filed from **foreign IP**; absence check >90 days/year → **7** dossiers qualified fraud, prejudice **€94,152**.
- **Identity + RIB:** Fake allocataire files from usurped IDs → neo-bank RIBs → **16** fraud dossiers, **€24,407**.
- **Source (primary CAF):** [Webinaire partenaires PDF 3 Jun 2024](https://www.caf.fr/sites/default/files/medias/521/partenaires/supports%20webinaires/Webinaire%20Partenaires%2003%2006%202024.pdf). **Gap:** individual judgments not named.

### Bonus detection (not a conviction) — CAF Haute-Garonne, May 2024
- **250** false RSA dossiers (identity + FR address, money abroad) since Feb 2024; **€17,000** paid then lost; **€900,000** RSA payment **avoided**; 155 further targets.
- **Source:** [Actu Toulouse](https://actu.fr/occitanie/toulouse_31555/toulouse-dans-le-viseur-de-la-caf-de-haute-garonne-ces-fraudeurs-d-un-nouveau-genre_61124722.html) quoting CAF31 director / finance director. **Not a court case.**
