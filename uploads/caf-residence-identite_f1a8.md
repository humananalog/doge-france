## 1) Lead — concrete cases / official pattern findings

1. **TJ Nice, 25 Apr 2025 — multi-CAF identity usurpation (RSA)**  
   - **Who/what:** Principal + accomplice; false IDs (incl. sports personalities per CNAF); forged docs; fake online RSA files.  
   - **Scale:** **29 CAF**; CAF Var DP: **74** false RSA dossiers, prejudice **>€45,000**; CNAF national DP: prejudice **€210,000**.  
   - **Outcome:** Principal **4 years firm** + *mandat de dépôt*; accomplice **6 months suspended**; judgment to be posted in affected CAF for 2 months + regional press publication.  
   - **Sources:** [CNAF DP fraude 2025](https://www.caf.fr/sites/default/files/medias/cnaf/Nous_connaitre/Presse/2026/260415%20DP%20Lutte%20contre%20la%20fraude.pdf) (example box); [CAF Var DP contrôles 2025](https://caf.fr/sites/default/files/medias/831/Presse/DP_controles_2025.pdf) (“Exemple de fraude à enjeux : usurpation d’identité”, judgment date **25/04/2025**); also [CNAF DP May 2025](https://www.caf.fr/sites/default/files/medias/cnaf/250505%20DPLa%20lutte%20contre%20la%20fraude%20%C3%A0%20la%20Caf%20%C3%A9volue%20avec%20la%20solidarit%C3%A9%20%C3%A0%20la%20source%20et%20le%20SNLFE.pdf).  
   - **Gap:** Full judgment PDF not on justice.fr from this dig; **€45k vs €210k** — cite both CAF texts; do not reconcile without judgment.

2. **TJ Nanterre — “Trafalgar” (audience 9 Jun 2026) — industrial multi-CAF identity ring**  
   - **Who/what:** SNLFE + multi-CAF signals; identity usurpation; serial fake online RSA dossiers; masked connections; payment transit circuits.  
   - **Scale:** **1,018 dossiers** in **10 months** across **75 CAF** (incl. CAF73); diverted or attempted **>€400,000**.  
   - **Outcome:** **M. Y** — 3 years (of which 2 suspended), €6,000 fine, 5 years ineligibility, joint civil liability; **M. X** — 18 months (12 suspended), €4,000 fine, 3 years ineligibility, joint civil liability.  
   - **Source:** [CAF Savoie official page](https://caf.fr/allocataires/caf-de-la-savoie/actualites-departementales/condamnation-suite-identification-de-fraude) (19 Jun 2026).  
   - **Gap:** No judgment PDF retrieved; defendants anonymised on CAF page.

3. **Paris TJ, 2025 — identity usurpation (CNAF-cited)**  
   - **Outcome:** **3 years firm** (CNAF: “condamnation à 3 ans de prison ferme”).  
   - **Source:** [CNAF DP May 2025](https://www.caf.fr/sites/default/files/medias/cnaf/250505%20DPLa%20lutte%20contre%20la%20fraude%20%C3%A0%20la%20Caf%20%C3%A9volue%20avec%20la%20solidarit%C3%A9%20%C3%A0%20la%20source%20et%20le%20SNLFE.pdf) p.6 area.  
   - **Gap:** No €, case name, or judgment URL in CNAF text. Treat as official pattern cite only until judgment found.

4. **Conseil d’État, 28 Nov 2025, n° 495335 — false identity bars all CAF rights**  
   - **Who/what:** CAF Vienne reclaim vs Mme C… (false Azerbaijani ID since entry 2004); APL + PPA + aide exceptionnelle **€17,274.83** (1 Jan 2017–30 Sep 2019); informed by préfet 1 Aug 2019.  
   - **Outcome:** CE quashes TA Poitiers 26 Apr 2024; reclaim stands; under **CSS L. 161-1-4**, false identity → **no benefit entitlement**; no duty to reassess under true identity before recovery.  
   - **Sources:** Decision text via [LibreJustice CE 495335](https://librejustice.fr/decision/fr/7EKqdL1qF93J); extract `/workspace/doge-france/sources/ce-495335-extract.txt`. Open official CE/Legifrance in browser (Ariane).

5. **TJ Alès, 14 May 2024 — landlord housing-aid fraud (non-decent bungalows)**  
   - **Who/what:** Sleep merchant; leisure bungalows declared as opening APL rights despite failing decency norms.  
   - **Scale:** **23** allocataire dossiers; housing aid diverted **>€124,000**.  
   - **Outcome:** Escroquerie; **€126,538** damages to CAF Gard; **2 years suspended** + **€150,000** fine.  
   - **Source:** [CAF Gard / CNAF professionals page](https://www.caf.fr/professionnels/presse/publications/la-plus-grosse-escroquerie-aux-prestations-gagnee-en-1ere-instance-par-la-caf-du-gard) (10 Jun 2024).  
   - **Note:** Not classic “résidence fictive of allocataire”; it is **false housing eligibility / false declaration to unlock APL** — keep in housing-document typology.

6. **Official pattern — SNLFE judicial pipeline (identity / docs)**  
   - As of CNAF DP 2025: SNLFE in **co-saisine on 12** judicial cases nationally; **5** of them = **identity usurpation and/or document fraud** hitting several CAF; prejudice **up to €400,000** per that passage.  
   - **Source:** [CNAF DP 2025](https://www.caf.fr/sites/default/files/medias/cnaf/Nous_connaitre/Presse/2026/260415%20DP%20Lutte%20contre%20la%20fraude.pdf) pp.7–8.

7. **Official pattern — “fraude à la résidence” scale (Branche Famille)**  
   - CNAF/SNLFE: résidence fraud typology already **>€123m** financial impact for Branche Famille; 2026 targeting priority.  
   - **Source:** same DP 2025 p.7 (Desmoulins Q&A).  
   - **Gap:** Not broken into fictive address vs prolonged stay abroad vs false lease in the published figure.

8. **CAF Var — résidence control exemplar (San Francisco)**  
   - Allocataire on RSA; bank ops abroad in USD; partners confirm permanent residence San Francisco; residence condition for RSA limited to **92 days**/year abroad.  
   - Prejudice **€7,837.72** (of which RSA €7,685.27); penalty **€1,175**.  
   - **Source:** [CAF Var DP fraude 2024](https://www.caf.fr/sites/default/files/medias/831/Presse/DP_Controle_fraude_2024.pdf) annex “Contrôle de résidence”.  
   - **Gap:** Administrative sanction case in DP annex — not a published TJ judgment.

TJ Draguignan (~Oct 2025) three brothers / Var CAF — false leases + children abroad; press (Nice-Matin / Midi Libre) cite ~€150k total, Nouri 18 months suspended + €10k fine + €37k still owed. **No CAF primary judgment post found this pass.**

## 2) Typology (with cited examples)

| Mode | What it is (operational) | Cited example |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Résidence fictive / false residence condition** | Claim French/stable residence (or eligible housing) while living elsewhere, using false address, or exceeding legal absence; or false leases/quittances | CAF Var annex San Francisco RSA case ([Var DP 2024](https://www.caf.fr/sites/default/files/medias/831/Presse/DP_Controle_fraude_2024.pdf)); SNLFE names *fraude à la résidence* as target ([CNAF DP 2025](https://www.caf.fr/sites/default/files/medias/cnaf/Nous_connaitre/Presse/2026/260415%20DP%20Lutte%20contre%20la%20fraude.pdf)); gov plan axis in [Var DP 2024](https://www.caf.fr/sites/default/files/medias/831/Presse/DP_Controle_fraude_2024.pdf) p.3 |
| **B. Usurpation d’identité** | Steal or fabricate identity to open CAF rights (often RSA online) | Nice TJ 25/04/2025 ([CNAF](https://www.caf.fr/sites/default/files/medias/cnaf/Nous_connaitre/Presse/2026/260415%20DP%20Lutte%20contre%20la%20fraude.pdf) + [Var 2025](https://caf.fr/sites/default/files/medias/831/Presse/DP_controles_2025.pdf)); Trafalgar Nanterre ([CAF73](https://caf.fr/allocataires/caf-de-la-savoie/actualites-departementales/condamnation-suite-identification-de-fraude)); CE 495335 civil reclaim doctrine ([LibreJustice](https://librejustice.fr/decision/fr/7EKqdL1qF93J)) |
| **C. Multi-CAF / multi-dossier** | Same scheme hits many caisses (serial dossiers) | Nice **29 CAF** / 74 dossiers; Trafalgar **75 CAF** / **1,018** dossiers — same sources as above |
| **D. False documents** | Forged CNI, leases, quittances, supporting papers | Nice (documents falsifiés — CNAF); Alès landlord bungalows / APL ([CAF Gard](https://www.caf.fr/professionnels/presse/publications/la-plus-grosse-escroquerie-aux-prestations-gagnee-en-1ere-instance-par-la-caf-du-gard)); SNLFE *fraude documentaire* target list (CNAF DP 2025) |
| **E. Related: RIB / account hijack** | Change victim’s RIB to neo-bank (often after credential theft) | Described as enjeux mode in [Var DP 2024](https://www.caf.fr/sites/default/files/medias/831/Presse/DP_Controle_fraude_2024.pdf) (OTP / fraudulent RIB base); **no single TJ cite in this memo** — gap |
